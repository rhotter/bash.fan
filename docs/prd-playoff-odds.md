## 1. Executive Summary

For years, Bay Area Street Hockey (BASH) players and captains tracked the late-season playoff race on **Sports Club Stats** (`sportsclubstats.com`), checking two signature views every week:
1. **"Chance to Make Playoffs / Chance to Finish Regular Season at Seed"** — a clean probability matrix showing each team's playoff odds, seed-by-seed odds, average seed, and week-over-week movement.
2. **"Big Games (Who Should We Root For?)"** — showing exactly how every team's playoff odds and projected seed shift depending on who wins each game in the upcoming matchday/week (including third-party teams affected by scoreboard watching!).

Now that Sports Club Stats is offline, this PRD defines a native, modern **Playoff Odds Predictor** built directly into the BASH website—faithfully preserving what made Sports Club Stats fun and intuitive while upgrading the underlying math for BASH's exact 3-2-1 scoring system, official Rule 213 tiebreakers, and empirically calibrated street hockey probabilities.

## 2. Goals & Non-Goals

### 2.1 Goals
1. **Playoff & Seed Probability Table**: Display every team's Playoff Qualification %, exact clinch/elimination status (`In` / `Out`), week-over-week delta (`Change`), full Seed `1..N` probability distribution with clear playoff & direct-bye cut lines, `Avg Seed`, `Strength`, and `RPI`.
2. **Big Games ("Who Should We Root For?")**: Automatically rank upcoming games by total playoff impact and show per-outcome conditional Playoff % and Avg Seed across regulation and OT/SO results (`Away Reg`, `Away OT/SO`, `Home OT/SO`, `Home Reg`), surfacing both participating teams *and* any scoreboard-watching team whose odds move meaningfully (`≥ 0.3%`).
3. **Interactive Team Drill-Down**: Clicking any team reveals:
   - **Playoff Odds Timeline** across every completed matchday in the season.
   - **"What If" ("How They Need to Finish") Table** showing final points/records vs. playoff & seed odds (using unbiased tail probing).
   - **Team-Filtered Big Games** showing every upcoming matchup that moves that team's odds.
4. **Interactive "What-If" Game Picker**: Let players toggle hypothetical winners on the upcoming matchday's games and immediately see the entire Playoff Odds table and Seed matrix recalculate live in the browser!
5. **Mode Transparency & Calibration**: Default to **Weighted (BASH Calibrated)**—proven in our 2,126-game backtest to outperform NHL defaults and raw `PTS%`—while letting users switch with one click to **50/50** (pure schedule/coin-flip math, like Sports Club Stats's `2.html` view) or **SCS Classic** (`Pts% + p225` regression, for nostalgic exact parity).
6. **Zero-Friction Navigation & Mobile Polish**: Integrate seamlessly as a 3rd view inside `/standings` (`Standings` | `Head to Head` | `Playoff Odds` via `/standings?view=odds`) AND expose a clean direct route `/playoff-odds` for easy sharing in team chats and social links.

### 2.2 Non-Goals (Phase 1)
- **Playoff Bracket Win / Championship Odds**: Phase 1 focuses exclusively on regular-season playoff qualification and regular-season seeding (1st through $N$th). Postseason series odds can be layered on later.
- **Roster / Attendance-Adjusted Pre-Game Projections**: Phase 1 uses game results and goal differentials (universally available across all historical and active seasons). Shot-share (`SF%`), `PDO`, and attendance-adjusted strength are slated for Phase 2.
- **Modifying Official Standings Table (`StandingsTab`)**: The existing `Standings` tab remains untouched; official Rule 213 tiebreakers power the Playoff Odds simulator.

## 3. High-Level Statistical Methodology & Outcomes

*(Full empirical calibration and historical 2018–2019 validation are documented in [research-sports-club-stats.md](./research-sports-club-stats.md).)*

### 3.1 Simulation Model & Modes

Every season snapshot is generated by running **100,000 deterministic Monte Carlo simulations** of all remaining regular-season games (seeded deterministically from the exact set of completed scores so results never jitter on refresh):

| Mode | Team Strength Estimator | Shrinkage Toward `.500` | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weighted (Default)** | **BASH-Calibrated PythagenPuck**: $S_{\text{raw}} = \frac{\text{GFg}^E}{\text{GFg}^E + \text{GAg}^E}$ where $E = (\text{GFg} + \text{GAg})^P$ (`P = 0.22` Fall, `P = 0.37` Summer) | Empirical Bayes: $S = \frac{\text{GP} \cdot S_{\text{raw}} + C \cdot 0.5}{\text{GP} + C}$ (`C = 10` Fall, `C = 6` Summer) | Highest out-of-sample accuracy across 2,126 BASH games (`Brier 0.2369` Fall, `0.2264` Summer); strips out 1-goal/OT luck while preventing small-sample overconfidence. |
| **50/50 (Coin Flip)** | Every team rated `0.500` | N/A | Pure schedule/standings math baseline (classic Sports Club Stats `2.html` view). Answers *"What are our odds if every remaining game is a coin flip?"* |
| **SCS Classic** | `PercentOfPointsWon` = $\frac{\text{PTS}}{3 \times \text{GP}}$ | Ken Roberts's exact `p225` piecewise regression (10% floor) | Exact historical parity with Sports Club Stats's 2018–2019 BASH page (reproduces archived odds within `0.1%`). |

### 3.2 Game Outcome Model

For every remaining regular-season game between Away team $A$ and Home team $H$:
1. **Win Probability (Log5, zero home-ice advantage)**:
   $$P(H \text{ wins}) = \frac{S_H (1 - S_A)}{S_H (1 - S_A) + S_A (1 - S_H)}$$
   Empirical BASH Home Win% is `49.3%` (single outdoor venue at James Lick Middle School), so `HFA = 0.50`.
2. **Regulation vs. OT vs. Shootout Split (4/6-Outcome BASH 3-2-1 System)**:
   - Calibrated extra-time rate: **12.0% Fall** (`7.5%` OT + `4.5%` SO), **9.0% Summer** (`5.5%` OT + `3.5%` SO), scaled smoothly so heavily lopsided matchups go to OT less often than evenly matched games (consistent with Ryder's Competing Poisson).
   - **Points awarded**: Regulation win = `3 / 0`; OT win = `2 / 1` (+1 `ROW` to winner); SO win = `2 / 1` (0 `ROW` to winner, per Rule 213).
   - **Simulated goal margin**: Drawn from calibrated BASH margin distributions (non-OT winner +1 to +6, OT/SO +1) so Goal Differential (`GD`) and Goals For (`GF`) tiebreakers resolve realistically.

### 3.3 Official BASH Tiebreakers (Rule 213) & Qualification Formats (Rules 412 & 219)

At the end of every simulated season, teams are ranked by official **BASH Rule 213**:
1. **Points (`PTS`)**
2. **ROW** (Regulation + Overtime Wins — shootout wins excluded)
3. **Head-to-Head (`H2H`)**: Percentage of available points earned in games among the tied teams (supports both 2-way and 3+-way ties; iteratively re-evaluates narrowed subgroups)
4. **Goal Differential (`GD`)**
5. **Goals For (`GF`)** → **Draw Lots** (per-iteration random tiebreak)

Qualification spots resolve automatically per season configuration and rulebook:
- **7-Team Fall** (e.g., `bash-2026-2027`, `playoffTeams = 5`): **Top 5 make playoffs** — Seeds `1–3` earn a **Direct Semifinal Bye**; Seeds `4–5` play the **4v5 Play-In** game.
- **6-Team Fall**: **Top 4 make playoffs** directly to Semifinals.
- **Summer (Rule 219)**: **All teams qualify** — the UI automatically adapts its copy and column framing to emphasize **Seed 1–N probabilities** and top-seed/bye races instead of showing trivial `100%` qualification alarms.

### 3.4 Exact Clinch / Elimination Guardrail

Following Ken Roberts's famous 2009 Senators lesson (`p270`), finite Monte Carlo `100.0%` or `0.0%` is **never** labeled `In` or `Out` unless verified mathematically:
- **Mathematically Clinched (`In`)**: Even if the team loses every remaining game in regulation (`0 pts`) and every rival below maximizes points, at most `playoffSpots - 1` teams can pass it (accounting for shared remaining games between rivals!).
- **Mathematically Eliminated (`Out`)**: Even if the team wins every remaining game in regulation (`+3 pts/game`), at least `playoffSpots` teams already exceed or are guaranteed to exceed its maximum possible points.
- **Unverified Extremes**: Displayed honestly as `>99.9%` (not yet clinched) or `<0.1%` (still mathematically alive!).

### 3.5 Big Games Single-Pass Conditional Accounting

During the exact same 100,000-iteration simulation pass (Ken Roberts's Nov 2009 `#10964` breakthrough), every upcoming game in the active window (default: **Next Matchday**, toggleable to **Next 2 Weeks**) accumulates conditional counts across 4 scoring-distinct outcomes:
- **Away Reg (`3–0`)**
- **Away OT/SO (`2–1`)**
- **Home OT/SO (`1–2`)**
- **Home Reg (`0–3`)**

*(Note: Internally the simulator tracks OT vs. SO separately for exact `ROW` tiebreakers, while presenting the 4 points-distinct columns cleanly in the UI so users aren't overwhelmed by near-identical OT and SO columns—with a tooltip explaining that OT wins count toward `ROW` while SO wins do not!)*

For each game $g$:
- **Total Playoff Swing**: $\sum_{t} \left(\max_{o} P(\text{team } t \text{ makes playoffs} \mid g = o) - \min_{o} P(\text{team } t \text{ makes playoffs} \mid g = o)\right)$.
- **Tiebreak / All-In Fallback Ranking** (Summer or late-season clinched games): Sorted by total **Avg Seed Swing** $\sum_{t} (\max_o E[\text{seed}_t \mid o] - \min_o E[\text{seed}_t \mid o])$.
- **Affected Teams Displayed**: Always include both participating teams, PLUS any third-party team whose conditional playoff swing is `≥ 0.3%` (or `≥ 0.15` avg seed swing in Summer / clinched races).
- **Sample-Size Flag (`*`)**: If any outcome occurred in fewer than 100 iterations, prefix its conditional value with `*` and footnote low-sample uncertainty.

### 3.6 "What If" ("How They Need to Finish") & Unbiased Tail Probing

To answer *"What does our team need to do down the stretch to make playoffs?"*, each team's detail view displays a table of final remaining records (or point buckets when $> 12$ remaining games produce too many unique records) showing `Remaining Record`, `Final PTS`, `Playoff %`, `Best–Worst Seed`, and `Sim Count`.

Crucially, honoring the `sidereal` `#10674` fix from Ken Roberts's blog, extreme win-out and lose-out scenarios are simulated in a **separate isolated tail-probing pass** (~1,000 runs per extreme per team) so they populate rare tail rows cleanly **without ever biasing the main playoff probabilities**.

## 4. UX & Interaction Design

### 4.1 Information Architecture & Entry Points

1. **Primary Surface — `/standings?view=odds`**:
   - Adds a 3rd top-level pill tab alongside `Standings` and `Head to Head` in `StandingsContent`:
     `Standings` | `Head to Head` | `Playoff Odds`
   - Uses shallow URL state synchronization (`window.history.replaceState`) preserving `?season=<id>&view=odds&mode=weighted|50_50|classic&team=<slug>` with zero unnecessary Server Component round-trips.
2. **Direct Shareable Route — `/playoff-odds`**:
   - Dedicated App Router page (`app/playoff-odds/page.tsx`) with SEO metadata and `loading.tsx`, rendering the exact same Playoff Odds experience (highlighted under `Standings` in `SiteHeader` so the 4-item header stays uncluttered).
   - Allows captains to share `https://.../playoff-odds` directly in group chats.

### 4.2 Visual Hierarchy & BASH Style Guide Compliance

Strict adherence to `AGENTS.md` and `modern-web-guidance` (`content-first-noise-reduction`, `responsive-table`, `tabs`):
- **Neutral Theme Tokens First**: Use `bg-background`, `bg-card`, `bg-muted`, `border-border`, `text-foreground`, and `text-muted-foreground` for tables, headers, and controls. Reserve `text-primary` (BASH orange) for primary CTAs (e.g., active Interactive What-If banner / Reset button).
- **No All-Caps Titles**: Section headings use clean sentence/title case (`Playoff odds & seed projections`, `Big games — who to root for`); only compact tabular column abbreviations (`GP`, `PTS`, `GD`, `RPI`, `GIH`) use small uppercase labels matching `StandingsTab`.
- **Semantic Probability & Delta Accents**:
  - Soft, accessible probability heat tints in the Seed `1..N` columns (neutral muted shading scaling with probability intensity, zero neon clutter).
  - Subtle semantic delta colors (`emerald-700` for positive playoff swing / rooting upside, `rose-700` for negative swing) with explicit `+`/`-` signs so color is never the sole indicator (WCAG AA contrast on light surface).
  - Clean vertical **Playoff Cut Line** (and subtle dashed **Direct Bye Cut Line** between Seeds 3 & 4 in 7-team 5-spot Fall seasons!) separating qualifying seeds from eliminated seeds.

### 4.3 Page Layout & Sections

#### Section 1: Compact Control Bar & Season Context Strip
- **Left**: Season context summary (e.g., `BASH 2026-2027 · 15 of 63 games played · Top 5 qualify (1–3 bye, 4v5 play-in)`) + last-updated timestamp & simulation count (`100,000 sims`).
- **Right**: Segmented **Model Mode** control (`Weighted` | `50/50` | `SCS Classic`) using accessible ARIA radiogroup/tablist semantics.
- If the user has locked hypothetical winners in **What-If Mode**, a clean dismissible banner appears right above the table: *"What-If scenario active (2 games locked) — showing live projected odds"* with a one-click **Reset picks** CTA.

#### Section 2: Playoff Odds & Seed Distribution Table (Signature Deliverable #1)
- **Sorting**: Default sorted by `Playoff % (desc) → Avg Seed (asc) → PTS (desc) → Rule 213 tiebreak`, with sortable column headers (`Playoff %`, `PTS`, `Avg Seed`, `Strength`, `RPI`).
- **Columns**:
  - **Team** (sticky left column on mobile, matching `StandingsTab`): Rank `#`, `<TeamLogo size={28} />`, team name (`IntentLink` to `/team/[slug]`), and expand chevron button to open Team Drill-Down.
  - **Record & Standings Context**: `PTS`, `GP` (with `GIH` tooltip/subtext), `Record` (`W-OTW-OTL-L`), `GD` (`+/-`).
  - **Playoff %**: Prominent tabular figure (`In`, `Out`, `>99.9%`, `<0.1%`, or `89.6%`) with inline mini progress bar and **matchday-over-matchday `Change`** badge (`+4.2%`, `-1.8%`) comparing against the previous completed matchday! *(In Summer seasons where all teams make playoffs, this column smoothly transitions to **Top-2 / #1 Seed %** or **Bye %** with a clear "All teams qualify" subtitle so there's zero wasted space!)*
  - **Seed 1 .. N Matrix** (grouped under spanning `<th colSpan={N}>Projected Finish Seed (%)</th>`): Each cell displays integer `%` (or `—` for `0%`, `<1` for `0.1–0.4%`) with subtle proportional background fill. Vertical divider between Seed 3 & Seed 4 (dashed, `Bye`) and thicker cut line after Seed `playoffSpots` (solid, `Playoff cut`).
  - **Avg Seed**: 1-decimal expected finish (e.g. `2.4`) + subtle delta vs. prior matchday.
  - **Strength** (hidden on `< md`, available in Team Drill-Down): Regressed `.000`–`1.000` rating (e.g., `.612`), with hover/focus tooltip contrasting **PythagenPuck Expected Win%** vs. actual **Pts%** ("Luck" indicator).
  - **RPI** (hidden on `< lg`, available in Team Drill-Down): `1..N` rank with tooltip showing raw RPI value.
- **Responsive Mobile Behavior** (`< 640px`):
  - Clean segmented sub-toggle above table on mobile (`Seeds` | `Details`) OR sticky Team column + horizontal scroll with smooth edge fade (`overflow-x-auto`), so mobile users immediately see `Team · PTS · Playoff % · Seed 1–N` without cramped columns.
- **Row Interaction**: Clicking anywhere on a team row (outside the team name link) expands/opens the **Team Drill-Down** panel for that team!

#### Section 3: Big Games — "Who Should We Root For?" (Signature Deliverable #2)
- **Header**: Title `Big games — who to root for`, scope toggle (`Next matchday` default, e.g. `Sat Nov 14` | `Next 2 weeks`), and optional team filter pill (`All teams` or filter to a single team).
- **Per-Game Cards / Compact Matrix** (ordered by highest total playoff swing):
  - **Game Header Bar**:
    - Matchup with logos (`Seals @ Loons`), date & time (`Sat Nov 14 · 9:00am`), pre-game win probability pill (`Seals 42% · Loons 58%`), `IntentLink` to `/game/[id]`, and total playoff swing badge (`14.8% swing`).
    - **Interactive What-If Picker Strip**: 4 compact pill buttons (`Away Reg`, `Away OT`, `Home OT`, `Home Reg`) right in the game card header—clicking any outcome locks that game result and immediately re-simulates the entire page in ~25 ms! Clicking again unlocks it.
  - **4-Outcome Conditional Impact Table**:
    - Rows: Each affected team (both playing teams first, then any third-party scoreboard-watching teams with `≥ 0.3%` swing, tagged subtly as `Watching`).
    - Columns: `Team` | `Current` | `Away Reg (3-0)` | `Away OT/SO (2-1)` | `Home OT/SO (1-2)` | `Home Reg (0-3)`.
    - Cells: Conditional Playoff % (and subtle `+/-` delta vs. current; in clinched/Summer matchups, displays Conditional Avg Seed prominently!). The best outcome for each team is subtly highlighted (`emerald` tint + subtle ring) so players see at a glance *"Who do I root for?"*.
- **Footer Note**: *"Outcomes marked `*` occurred in fewer than 100 simulated seasons. Playoff swings from separate games on the same day are not strictly additive."*

#### Section 4: Team Drill-Down Panel (Inline Expandable / Drawer)
When a user selects a team from the main table (or URL `?team=<slug>`):
1. **Plain-English Summary Strip**: e.g., *"Landsharks hold an 89.6% chance to make the playoffs (projected #5 seed, range #3–#7). Last game: L vs Rink Rats 1–3. Next: Sat Nov 21 vs Seals."*
2. **Playoff Odds Timeline Chart** (`Recharts` responsive line chart):
   - X-axis: Matchday dates across the season (`Oct 10`, `Oct 17`, ..., `Nov 7`).
   - Y-axis: Playoff % (`0%`–`100%`) — or Projected Avg Seed (inverted `1` at top) toggleable!
   - Also allows comparing 2–3 teams or hovering each matchday to see the exact score that moved the needle.
3. **"What If" ("How They Need to Finish") Table**:
   - Lists achievable remaining point totals / record buckets (`18–21 pts`, `15–17 pts`, ..., `0–2 pts`, or exact `W-L` rows when `≤ 6` games remain) with `Final PTS`, `Playoff %`, `Projected Seed`, and `Sim Count` (with unbiased tail-probing footnote when tail rows come from forced probes).
4. **Team-Specific Upcoming Schedule & Rooting Guide**: Upcoming games ranked by how strongly they move *this specific team's* odds.

#### Section 5: Methodology & Rules Disclosure
- Native HTML `<details>` disclosure block at the bottom of the page (`How playoff odds are calculated`), keeping the main viewport clean and uncluttered (`content-first-noise-reduction`) while providing full transparency:
  - Explains Weighted (BASH-calibrated PythagenPuck + Empirical Bayes shrinkage + Log5) vs. 50/50 vs. SCS Classic.
  - Documents empirical BASH calibration (`HFA = 50%`, `12%` Fall / `9%` Summer OT/SO rate, `P = 0.22` / `0.37`).
  - Lists official Rule 213 tiebreakers (`PTS → ROW → H2H pts% → GD → GF`).
  - Credits Ken Roberts (Sports Club Stats), Alan Ryder (*Win Probabilities*), and Tom Tippett (*Log5*).

## 5. Edge Cases & Seasonal States

| State | Expected Product Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Pre-season / 0 games played** | Every team starts at `.500` strength; Weighted and 50/50 produce identical symmetric odds (`~playoffSpots / N`), Big Games shows Opening Day matchups, Timeline shows initial pre-season baseline. |
| **Early season (1–4 GP)** | Empirical Bayes shrinkage (`C = 10` Fall / `6` Summer) keeps strengths anchored near `.500` so a `2-0` team isn't falsely projected at `99%`. |
| **Mid-season active (`bash-2026-2027`, 15/63 final)** | Full experience: live Playoff %, Seed 1–7 matrix, Play-In cut line, matchday-over-matchday `Change`, Big Games for upcoming Saturday, 5-matchday Timeline, What-If picker. |
| **Completed historical season (e.g., `2018-2019`, `2025-2026`)** | Final standings show `In` (`100%`) / `Out` (`0%`) at actual finishing seeds, Big Games shows *"Regular season complete — no remaining games"*, AND **Playoff Odds Timeline** still renders the entire season's dramatic odds trajectory week by week! |
| **Truncated / cancelled season (`2019-2020`, 60/63 played, `status = completed`)** | Recognizes `status === "completed"` and displays final recorded standings + season timeline with a note that the regular season ended early (3 games unplayed). |
| **Summer season (`playoffSpots === teamCount`)** | Switches primary highlight from qualification % to **Seed 1–N & #1 Seed / Bye odds**; Big Games ranks by Avg Seed swing so every game remains meaningful. |
| **Forfeits & Double Forfeits** | Properly applies Rule 208 (`5–0` 3-pt win for single forfeit; `0–5` 0-pt loss for both teams on double forfeit) in standings, tiebreakers, and goal differential (excluding forfeits from PythagenPuck scoring efficiency if desired, or keeping consistent with official GF/GA—documented clearly in TDD). |
| **All-Time Season Selector (`?season=all`)** | Displays the same clear message as Standings (*"Playoff odds require a specific season"*). |

## 6. Verification & Success Criteria

1. **Mathematical Accuracy**:
   - $\sum_t P(\text{playoffs}_t) = \text{playoffSpots} \times 100\%$ (within floating-point rounding) and $\sum_t P(\text{seed}_{t,k}) = 100\%$ for every seed $k$.
   - Exact reproduction of archived 2018–2019 Sports Club Stats BASH snapshot (`±0.5%`) in `SCS Classic` mode.
   - Exact Rule 213 tiebreaker behavior verified on unit tests covering `ROW` (excluding SO wins) and 2-way / 3-way `H2H` percentage ties.
2. **Performance**:
   - Server computation of 100,000 iterations completes in `< 250 ms` on cold cache and `< 30 ms` on cache hit (`unstable_cache`).
   - Client-side Interactive What-If re-simulation (20,000 iterations) completes in `< 50 ms` for instant UI feedback.
3. **Usability & Accessibility**:
   - Clean, readable layout verified on both Desktop (`1280px`) and Mobile (`390px`) viewports without clipped text or broken sticky columns.
   - Keyboard-navigable tabs, sortable columns (`aria-sort`), semantic `<th scope>` headers, and native `<details>` methodology disclosure.
