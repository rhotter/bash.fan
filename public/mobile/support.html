BASH Hockey support

Follow Bay Area Street Hockey scores, schedules, standings, and player statistics on iPhone.

Scores and statistics

Use the season menu to choose a season. On Scores, choose a week to view its games. Tap a game, team, or player for details. Pull down to refresh. When offline, previously loaded scores and standings remain available.

League operator tools

Open the three-dot menu on Scores or a game to access Scorekeeper or Record game. These tools require the league admin passcode. Contact your league organizer for authorized access.

To record, select the camera side and quality, grant camera and microphone permission, and keep the app open. Use reliable Wi-Fi and power for long games. Uploads can continue in the background. Reopen the app to resume interrupted uploads.

App Review access

Choose Scorekeeper or Record game, enter the reviewer code supplied in App Review Information on the passcode screen, then tap Continue. Demo scorekeeping uses sample players and keeps changes in memory. Demo recording uses the camera but never streams or uploads footage. Play clips from Demo recorder, or manage them in Files → BASH → Recordings. A physical iPhone with a rear ultra-wide camera is required to record.

Get help or report content

Email raphael.hotter@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, and the game or issue involved. You can also report incorrect league records or request removal of footage. Never send your admin passcode.
