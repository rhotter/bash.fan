BASH Hockey privacy policy

Updated October 25, 2026. BASH Hockey is operated by Raffi Hotter for Bay Area Street Hockey. This policy covers the iPhone app and its league and recording services.

Browsing league information

You can browse scores, schedules, standings, and player statistics without creating an account. League records include player names, team assignments, game participation, and statistics. The app saves preferences and recent scores and standings on your device. Hosting providers may retain IP addresses and technical request logs to operate and protect the service.

League operator tools

Authorized operators can submit rosters, player names, scores, penalties, officials, and game notes. These submissions update league records and may appear publicly on BASH. Operator access is protected by a league passcode.

Recording games

If you use Record game, the app captures camera video and microphone audio after you grant permission. It saves clips on your phone and uploads them to BASH; a live feed is sent while streaming is connected. Footage can identify players and spectators and include their voices.

Recording services also store session and camera identifiers, device name, timestamps, recording and upload status, battery level, and connection or recording errors. This information supports recording, playback, camera synchronization, and troubleshooting. It can be associated with the recording device and footage.

Demo mode

App reviewers can enter a separate review code on the operator passcode screen to access sample tools. Sample scorekeeping stays in memory and does not update league records. Demo video and audio stay on the phone and are not streamed, uploaded, or used for telemetry. You can manage or delete demo clips in Files → BASH → Recordings.

Sharing and retention

Vercel hosts the service, Neon stores league and recording metadata, and Cloudflare processes video through R2 and Stream. Authorized league operators can access recordings. Footage associated with games may be published on BASH game pages. We do not sell this data or use it for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking.

League records and uploaded footage are retained as a league archive unless removed. Local clips are removed after the server verifies their upload; unverified clips remain available for retry. Camera recording stops when the app backgrounds, but uploads can continue.

Your choices

Only record where you have permission and notify participants that footage may be streamed or published. You can stop recording and revoke camera or microphone permission in iOS Settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of league records, footage, or recording metadata, email raphael.hotter@gmail.com with enough detail to locate the record. Never include the league passcode. We review requests and applicable retention obligations.
